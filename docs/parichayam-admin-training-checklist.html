Parichayam Admin Training Checklist

Use this as the practical handover sheet for new admin and operations staff.

1. Access and Readiness

  • Confirm access to admin panel, member dashboard, payment dashboard, OTP dashboard, and support mailbox.
  • Confirm who owns verification, payments, content, and agent payout approvals.
  • Confirm emergency escalation contact for auth, payment, or site outage issues.

2. Website Basics

  • Open index page and confirm all main buttons work.
  • Open auth page and confirm login, signup, and password reset are visible.
  • Test at least one successful sign-in using a real or staging user.

3. Registration and Trial Flow

  • Verify registration step 1 captures profile-for, name, mobile, email, and password.
  • Verify OTP requirement if OTP mode is enabled.
  • Verify trial signup works when no paid plan is selected.
  • Verify paid-plan signup redirects correctly to checkout.

4. Matchmaking Training

  • Admin understands that only opposite-gender profiles appear in normal match suggestions.
  • Admin understands same-gotra hard policy can fully block a profile.
  • Admin understands age fit is directional and rewards common marriage-age patterns.
  • Admin understands city, community, verification, completeness, and recent activity improve ranking.
  • Admin understands boost is a limited visibility bonus, not a full override of relevance.

5. Admin Panel Training

Pricing and Revenue

  • Change one membership price and verify the save path.
  • Review add-on prices and connect pack controls.

Dashboard Builder

  • Show how to enable or disable a member dashboard section.
  • Explain which sections are CORE and cannot be hidden.

Profile Form Builder

  • Show how to mark a field as shown, hidden, or required.
  • Explain that hiding too many important fields lowers match quality.

Verification Queue and User Access

  • Approve one Aadhaar verification and one income verification example.
  • Show how to activate an add-on or restrict a user if needed.

6. Daily Operations

  • Morning: new registrations, trial expiry watchlist, verification queue, payments, agent requests.
  • Afternoon: match-quality spot checks, incomplete-profile review, support follow-up.
  • End of day: revenue summary, settings review, backlog review, risk review.

7. Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Do not approve weak or fake profiles to increase counts.
  • Do not hide critical fields like city, community, education, profession, or income without reason.
  • Do not change OTP or payment settings without testing signup and checkout.
  • Do not enable strict same-gotra blocking unless data quality is clean.
  • Do not over-promise that every saved partner preference is strictly enforced today.
