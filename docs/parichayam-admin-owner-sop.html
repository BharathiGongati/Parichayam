Purpose of this SOP

This document explains how Parichayam works in business language so that a non-technical admin can run the platform, understand match generation, and know which admin controls affect live user behavior.

1. System Overview

Parichayam is a matrimony platform designed around Arya Vyshya matchmaking. It operates through four layers: public website, member dashboard, admin panel, and internal operations channels such as matchmaker and agent workflows.

Public LayerIndex page, trust-building, registration entry, and conversion.
Member LayerSignup, trial or paid plan, profile completion, matching, interests, shortlist, messages.
Operations LayerAdmin controls, pricing, verification, agents, reports, staff, and content governance.
Member DataStored in Firestore with profile, plan, verification, and ranking attributes.
IdentityHandled through Firebase Authentication with email/password and OTP support.
PaymentsHandled through backend-controlled payment order and verification flow.
MatchesGenerated by backend ranking and shown inside dashboard views.

Business Model Summary

  • Users register and begin on a free trial or move directly into a paid plan.
  • The platform encourages complete profiles, verification, and photos to improve trust and response quality.
  • Revenue comes from plans, add-ons, boost, connect packs, verification-related value, and agent channels.
  • Admin controls visibility, pricing, verification workflow, dashboard sections, and selected matchmaking behavior.

3. Page-Wise SOP

3.1 Index Page

Business purpose: top-of-funnel page that sells trust, community focus, and the reason to register.

  • Main actions: sign up, sign in, learn about the service, open agent enquiry, and access admin login.
  • Operational note: support contact details, trust claims, and CTA buttons must always be current.

3.2 Auth Page

Business purpose: login, signup, password reset, and plan selection.

  • Registration is two-step: account basics first, plan/trial next.
  • Mobile OTP can be required before signup continues.
  • If a paid plan is selected, account creation happens first and checkout follows.
  • If no paid plan is selected, the account begins on a trial plan.

3.3 Dashboard Page

Business purpose: the main member workspace where discovery, engagement, and monetization happen.

  • Main sections include Home, Today’s Matches, Search Partners, Shortlist, Interests, Messages, Notifications, Profile, Kundali Match, Psychology Profile, Matchmaker, Services, and Settings.
  • Home shows membership state, highlighted suggestions, and engagement widgets.
  • Today’s Matches is the main ranked match feed.
  • Search Partners is the manual exploration area.

4. Matchmaking Logic in Business Language

The current live backend follows a rule-and-score model. It removes invalid profiles first, then ranks the remaining candidates. This is safer and more realistic than random listing for a growing but still limited database.

4.1 Basic Match Rules

  1. Only opposite-gender profiles are considered.
  2. Hidden, blocked, suspended, deleted, or non-member accounts are excluded.
  3. The member cannot match with their own profile.
  4. If same-gotra policy is hard, same-gotra matches are fully blocked.
  5. The remaining candidates are scored and sorted.

4.3 Age Gap Logic Explained

The live backend uses a directional groom-older-by model. The strongest score is when the groom is around 2 to 5 years older than the bride.

  • Best bracket: groom older by 2 to 5 years
  • Good bracket: groom older by 0 to 7 years
  • Wider or reverse difference may still appear, but with lower ranking
Example A

Boy age 30, girl age 26. Groom is older by 4 years, so this receives a top age-fit score.

Example B

Girl age 27, boy age 30. Groom is older by 3 years, so this also receives a top age-fit score.

4.4 Education, Salary, Business Income, and Location

  • Education: Broadly similar education backgrounds rank higher.
  • Salary / business income: Better alignment improves order, but missing income usually lowers ranking instead of removing the profile.
  • Location: Same city is best, same state is next, same country is acceptable, long-distance is lower ranked.
  • Community: Exact community match gets the strongest score.

4.5 User Preferences vs Admin Rules

Current Business Truth

In the current live build, the system is stronger on actual profile facts and admin-controlled policy settings than on deep saved partner-preference enforcement. Partner-preference sections exist and are useful, but the live backend ranking today is mainly driven by profile data, trust, completeness, location, community, and kundali settings.

5. Admin Control Guide

5.1 How to Configure Matchmaking-Related Behavior

  1. Review Community Manager and keep only correct community labels.
  2. Review Gotra Manager and keep gotra spelling clean and standardized.
  3. Open kundali settings and confirm:
    • kundali enabled or disabled
    • same-gotra policy
    • who can access kundali: free, trial, paid
    • kundali weight in ranking
  4. Use Dashboard Builder to decide which matching-related sections members can see.
  5. Use Profile Form Builder to keep critical data fields visible and, where needed, required.
  6. Use User Access Control to activate add-ons like gotra block or boost for specific users.

5.2 How Admin Reviews and Approves Users

  1. New user completes registration and receives a member account.
  2. Admin reviews profile quality, photo quality, and completeness.
  3. Admin checks verification documents from the Verification Queue.
  4. Admin approves or rejects Aadhaar and income verification.
  5. Hidden, blocked, or suspended users should not remain visible in discovery.

5.3 What Happens When Admin Changes Rules

5.4 Admin Do's and Don'ts

Do
  • Keep city, community, gotra, education, and income data clean.
  • Push members to complete profile and upload clear photos.
  • Review verification queue every day.
  • Use boost as a limited visibility advantage, not as fake relevance.
  • Recheck important admin changes from a fresh session.
Do Not
  • Hide too many critical fields just to simplify onboarding.
  • Enable hard same-gotra blocking without clean gotra data.
  • Approve weak or fake profiles to inflate counts.
  • Promise families that every partner preference is fully enforced by the current system.
  • Change auth or payment settings without testing the user journey afterward.

7. Daily Operations SOP

Start of Day Checklist

  • Check new registrations and identify incomplete or suspicious profiles.
  • Check the verification queue and clear pending Aadhaar or income items.
  • Check trial-expiry watchlist and identify users nearing conversion deadline.
  • Check payment success, failed activations, and pending plan issues.
  • Check agent applications and payout requests.

Midday Operational Review

  • Spot-check 2 to 3 member dashboards and verify match quality.
  • Review whether city, community, and gotra values remain clean and consistent.
  • Review new uploaded photos and document quality if staff workflow requires it.
  • Check support issues from WhatsApp, phone, and email.

End of Day Review

  • Check revenue summary: memberships, add-ons, connect packs, boosts, verification value, and agent-related costs.
  • Check whether any major admin settings were changed and confirm the live effect.
  • Check staff, matchmaker, and verification workload balance.
  • Check whether any member complaints indicate bad match quality or trust problems.

Weekly Business Review

  • Trial-to-paid conversion rate
  • City-wise registration and paid conversion
  • Verification completion percentage
  • Boost sales versus actual engagement quality
  • Agent performance and payout accuracy
  • Member complaint trends and hidden-profile trends

8. Final Recommendations

  1. Keep the live backend match engine as the operational truth: eligibility first, score second, boost last.
  2. Upgrade saved partner preferences into stronger backend ranking signals in the next product phase.
  3. Expose age-gap bands as formal admin-controlled settings if the business wants more control.
  4. Maintain strict profile quality and verification discipline; this improves both trust and renewals.
  5. Use paid boost carefully: it should improve visibility, not destroy relevance.
  6. Before public scale-up, perform a final business QA sweep on signup, OTP, payment, profile save, verification, and Today’s Matches.
