Cancellation and Refund Policy

Effective date: March 30, 2026

1) Membership and Add-on Purchases

  • Once a membership plan or digital add-on is activated/used, it is generally non-refundable.
  • If activation has not happened due to a confirmed platform-side issue, a review may be considered.

2) Duplicate or Failed Payments

  • Duplicate charges or failed transactions can be raised for review.
  • After validation with payment records, eligible reversals/refunds may be processed.

3) Cancellation Requests

  • Cancellation requests must be submitted through official support channels listed on Contact Us.
  • Please include registered mobile/email, payment reference, date, and reason for faster handling.

4) Review Timelines

  • Initial review response target: 3-5 business days.
  • If approved, refund settlement timeline depends on payment provider/bank rails.

5) Admin Review and Exceptions

Parichayam may allow case-by-case exceptions where justified by verified transaction evidence, technical errors, or compliance requirements. Final decision follows internal review logs and payment verification records.
